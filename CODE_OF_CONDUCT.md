# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, protect private information, and focus disagreements on evidence and project outcomes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the contact methods available on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
